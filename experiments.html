EXPERIMENTS / EVOLUTION / APPARATUS

Two runs.
One apparatus.

A pre-registered 2×2 factorial over twelve harness instances, and a coupled follow-up that makes the boundary interaction estimable. Both execute real harnesses; neither is a field result, and neither estimates how model populations behave.

Evidence status: apparatus-confirmatory. Twelve configured instances of two templates, one model snapshot, and a frozen human-authority policy. The field experiment remains prospective.

What the apparatus holds fixed.

RUN 01 / PRE-REGISTERED / 10 ROUNDS

The frozen 2×2 run.

The confirmatory layer froze practical margins, the decision rule, and a human-authority policy before the matrices ran. An out-of-seam change is refused whether the replica is MU, MB, EU, or EB; the agent cannot approve its own exception. The evolution agent is one local model snapshot, reported by the CLI as deepseek/deepseek-flash.

Enforcement. Contract-preserving success is equal at 0.995 in both modes, and M produces 4.000 more unauthorized effects per replica than E (simultaneous 97.5% interval 4.000 to 4.000; margin 0.50). Round zero already separates the arms: M fails two of four probes and all four adversarial tasks before any evolution, so the main effect is a property of the runtime guard rather than accumulated change.

Why the interaction is zero here

The promotion gate runs the same held-out tasks in E and M, and the external guard acts only at runtime; it does not enter promotion. Under the frozen authority, the out-of-seam requests that produce drift in the pilot are rejected in every arm. The zero is a failed manipulation check, not evidence that boundaries add nothing.

03
RUN 02 / COUPLED FOLLOW-UP / 11 ROUNDS

Making the interaction estimable.

The frozen design cannot estimate the boundary interaction because the guard and the gate act on disjoint mechanisms. The coupled follow-up makes them share one obligation: the external guard's own protection policy, guard_policy.protected_actions. The guard enforces that policy at runtime; the gate compares a candidate policy with the immutable baseline contract and rejects any weakening. Round eleven is an in-seam request to stop blocking commands outside the allowlist while keeping workspace write containment. Filesystem containment stays an internal permission obligation; command allowlisting is enforced only by the guard, so the same promoted change has a runtime effect in E and none in M.

Result. The drift interaction is 0.972 (simultaneous 0.889 to 1.000; pair-clustered 95% 0.917 to 1.000) and the unauthorized-effects interaction is 0.972 with the same intervals, both above the frozen 0.50 margin. The boundary benefit is 1.000 in E and 0.028 in M for drift, and 0.972 versus 0.000 for unauthorized effects. Regression is descriptive and its interaction is 0.000.

Exposure audit. In every non-declined sample the round-eleven narrowing is promoted in EU, rejected in EB and MB with a recorded guard-policy mismatch, and promoted in MU. One of thirty-six round-eleven proposals was declined; its replicas remain in their arm as non-exposure rather than being dropped.

Read this estimate narrowly

The result is structural. It shows that the instrument can express a boundary-by-enforcement interaction when the two mechanisms share an obligation, and that the gate preserves the guard policy supplying it. It is not a field interaction, not a model-population estimate, and not a revision of the frozen confirmatory result. The revision that retargeted round eleven from write containment to command containment happened before any confirmatory matrix, is recorded in the protocol, and did not change the frozen margins.

04

What these experiments do not show.

  • They are not field tests. The cohort is 12 configured instances of two templates, not the frozen 120-slot field task bank.
  • They do not estimate model-population variation. The evolution agent is one model snapshot reported as deepseek/deepseek-flash.
  • They do not replace case-by-case human authority. The authority is a frozen policy that rejects out-of-seam changes.
  • The coupled estimate is structural. It demonstrates that the mechanism can be made estimable; it does not show that boundaries help in general.
  • No causal performance, safety, cost, or reliability benefit in real repositories follows from these runs.
05

Reproduce.

The experiment package is the companion pohe-experiments directory. Plans are fixed inputs: rerunning a sample replays them byte for byte, and the run tables are deterministic.

# Pre-registered confirmatory run
python3 runner/generate_online_plans.py --out-root results_confirmatory --samples 3 --workers 6
for s in 1 2 3; do
  python3 runner/run_phase3.py --plans results_confirmatory/plans_s$s \
    --out results_confirmatory/run_s$s --confirmatory \
    --authority-policy protocol/authority_policy.json
done
python3 analysis/pool_confirmatory.py --root results_confirmatory --samples 3
python3 analysis/confirmatory_decision.py --root results_confirmatory --samples 3

# Coupled follow-up
python3 runner/generate_coupled_round.py --samples 3 --workers 6
for s in 1 2 3; do
  python3 runner/run_phase3.py --plans results_coupled/plans_s$s \
    --templates templates_coupled \
    --sequence protocol/change_sequence_coupled.json \
    --confirmatory --coupled \
    --authority-policy protocol/authority_policy.json \
    --out results_coupled/run_s$s
done
python3 analysis/pool_confirmatory.py --root results_coupled --samples 3
python3 analysis/coupled_decision.py --root results_coupled --samples 3
python3 analysis/export_site_experiments.py

Outputs: results_confirmatory/decision.md and results_coupled/decision.md carry the pooled arm means, estimands, intervals, decisions, and exposure audits. The run manifests record protocol hashes and the authority scope.
