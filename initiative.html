POHE / OPEN INITIATIVE

A discipline for the
system around the model.

POHE is an open research and engineering initiative. We build shared vocabulary, evidence, review, and tooling for agent harnesses: the executable layer that assembles context, exposes tools, enforces permissions, maintains state, evaluates work, and decides when to stop.

01

Why an initiative

Models absorb scaffolding. They do not automatically absorb accountability.

As model capability improves, more context management, tool choice, and review can move into the model. Authorization, acceptance, audit, recovery, and termination remain system obligations: someone or something must establish who may act, what evidence counts as acceptance, what survives a failure, and when execution must stop.

In practice these obligations are often recorded only as component names or implementation details. Teams rediscover the same harness decisions under different labels, and a rewrite can silently remove the guarantee that made the original design safe. POHE exists to make those obligations durable, reviewable, and comparable through change.

01 / CONTRACT

Contract before label

A pattern name is only useful when it identifies the obligation that must hold and the variation that is permitted.

02 / EVIDENCE

Evidence before claim

A keyword match, a source-located exemplar, and a tested contract are different evidence levels. The initiative keeps them separate.

03 / AUTHORITY

Independent authority

The proposal, execution, acceptance, and audit paths should not collapse into one self-certifying loop.

04 / FALSIFICATION

Open falsification

Every research claim should name the observation that would weaken or reject it. H1–H3 are public research targets, not settled results.

03

How to take part

POHE becomes useful when teams can test it on their own harness and publish what survives.

  1. Map a responsibility. Pick one consequential harness obligation, name its join point, and state what may vary.
  2. Try the instrument. Run pohe-agent against a repository and separate retrieval cues from structural evidence.
  3. Review a record. Contribute an independent H2 judgment from the frozen source excerpts.
  4. Report a case. Open an issue with a field case, contract test, counterexample, or an implementation that the record fails to explain.
  5. Replicate a study. Test H1 persistence or H3 record value under a design that can falsify the claim.
04

Status and boundaries

The initiative is deliberately explicit about what is established, what is demonstrated in an apparatus, and what remains prospective.

  • The 52 records are a scoped candidate inventory, not a prevalence estimate.
  • Source anchors are worked exemplars, not evidence that other repositories conform to the proposed contract.
  • The H2 testbed demonstrates mechanisms under controlled software conditions; it is not a field estimate of effectiveness.
  • H1 and H3 remain prospective research programs, and independent human adjudication remains open.
  • The pattern records and diagrams describe proposed obligations; they do not certify any implementation.
